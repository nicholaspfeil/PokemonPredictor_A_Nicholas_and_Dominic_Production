Pokémon Predictor

Where's your collection heading? A machine-learning model reads months of real market history and forecasts where each card's value is going over the next two months.

A student machine-learning project — forecasts are offered for curiosity, not investing advice or any guarantee of a card's future value. Prices are genuinely hard to predict, and hype or reprints can move a card in ways no model foresees.
