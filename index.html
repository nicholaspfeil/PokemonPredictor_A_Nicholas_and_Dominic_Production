Pokémon Predictor
market edition

A price guide that looks ahead. A machine-learning model reads months of real market history and forecasts where each card's value is heading over the next two months.

Forecasts are estimates from a student machine-learning model, offered for curiosity — not investing advice, and not a guarantee of any card's future value. The model captures real but limited signal (cross-validated R² ≈ 0.16); card prices are genuinely hard to predict, and sudden hype or reprints can move a card in ways no model foresees.
